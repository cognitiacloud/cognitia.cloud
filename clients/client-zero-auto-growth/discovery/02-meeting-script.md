# Meeting Script — Auto Growth OS Discovery Call

A 30–40 minute discovery + presentation call. Goal: understand the dealership,
fill the questionnaire fields live, and present the Auto Growth OS as the obvious
next step. Tone: peer-to-peer operator, not pitchy. We diagnose before we prescribe.

> Guardrail reminder for the presenter: never promise sales, rankings, ROI, lead
> counts, financing terms, or trade-in values. Use the approved phrasing card at
> the end. See `../internal/guardrails.md`.

---

## 0. Before the call (2 min prep)

- Pull up their current website and one competitor.
- Have the questionnaire (`01-discovery-questionnaire.md`) and the Discovery
  Console open. You'll fill fields as they talk.
- Goal of the call in one line: *leave with a completed config and a booked
  next step (build kickoff or proposal review).*

## 1. Open (2–3 min)

> "Thanks for the time. Quick agenda: I want to understand how you sell cars
> today, where deals leak, and then show you exactly how we'd fix the biggest
> leak first. If it's a fit, we'll talk next steps; if it's not, I'll tell you
> straight. Sound good?"

- Confirm who's on the call and who signs off (field 30/38).
- "Before tools and websites — tell me about the business." → Section A.

## 2. Diagnose (10–12 min) — listen, tag fields

Work the questionnaire conversationally. You're hunting for **the leak**.

- Inventory & how it's published → Section B (`inventory_size`,
  `inventory_update_method`).
- "Walk me from *lead comes in* to *deal closed*." → Section E. **Listen for where
  it goes cold** (field 22). This is your wedge.
- Channels and response time → Section D. Ask the killer question:
  > "When someone messages you at 8pm on Marketplace, what happens?"
  Most dealers admit: nothing until morning. That's the leak.
- Goals → Section F (`primary_goal`).

**Reframe (the pivot):**
> "So you're already generating demand — calls, walk-ins, messages. The problem
> isn't getting attention, it's that some of the demand you've *already paid for*
> leaks out before anyone responds. That's fixable, and it's cheaper than buying
> more leads."

## 3. Present the Auto Growth OS (8–10 min)

Frame it as **one system, not a website**:

> "We don't sell you a website. We install an operating system for growth. Seven
> parts, but they work as one:"

1. **A site built to convert** — fast, mobile, every vehicle has its own page.
2. **Inventory that publishes itself** — stock flows to the site and your
   channels without manual re-entry; photos get a privacy/quality check before
   they go live.
3. **One inbox for every channel** — WhatsApp, phone, Marketplace, web form land
   in one place.
4. **An AI Sales Closer** — answers in seconds, day or night, qualifies the
   buyer, books the test drive, and hands the hot ones to your team. It always
   says it's an assistant and hands off the moment money or trade-in value comes
   up.
5. **A CRM-lite pipeline** — every lead tracked from new to won, nothing falls
   through.
6. **Found where buyers look** — search, AI answers, and your local map presence,
   done the right way.
7. **Proof you can see** — a dashboard of real numbers, reviewed with you on a
   set cadence.

> "All of it runs under Cognitia's control plane — the AI follows rules we set
> together, and every result is recorded so you can trust the numbers, not just
> take our word."

Tie back to **their** leak:
> "For you, the first win is [their `primary_goal`]. Here's the 30/60/90 we'd run."
> → walk the roadmap headline (`proposal/10-roadmap-30-60-90.md`).

## 4. Show the recommendation (3–4 min)

Open the Discovery Console with their fields filled in.

> "Based on what you just told me — [inventory size], [channels], [goal] — the
> system recommends the **[Launch/Growth/Scale]** package, with these modules
> turned on first. Notice it flags finance and trade-in for your sign-off — we
> never put a number or a promise in front of your customers without you
> approving it."

Let the tool do the talking. It makes the recommendation feel diagnosed, not sold.

## 5. Handle objections

| They say | You say (guardrail-safe) |
| --- | --- |
| "We tried an agency, got nothing." | "Most agencies sell traffic and disappear. We instrument the leak you already have and show you the numbers weekly. If it's not working, you'll see it before I do." |
| "How many sales will this get me?" | "I won't promise a number — anyone who does is guessing. What I'll promise is faster response, every lead tracked, and honest reporting. The leads you're already losing are the upside." |
| "Will I rank #1 on Google?" | "No one can promise a ranking. We do the work that makes you eligible to be found — proper pages, structured answers, local presence — and we report movement honestly." |
| "It's too expensive." | "Compare it to one lost deal a month. The system's job is to stop those losses. Let's look at which package fits your budget band." |
| "Can it do financing/trade-in quotes?" | "It *collects* that interest instantly so you never lose the lead — but a person on your team produces every number. We don't put rates or trade values in front of a customer without your approval." |
| "We're too busy to switch tools." | "We design around what you already use and keep. Nothing rips out on day one; we add the layer that stops the leaks." |

## 6. Close to next step (2 min)

Always close to a **specific** next action, never "think about it":

> "Two ways to go: I can send the proposal with this exact config and pricing for
> you to review with [signer], or — if you're ready — we book the build kickoff
> and I get the first win live in 30 days. Which fits better?"

- Confirm the config is captured.
- Book the next meeting on the call.
- Send proposal + recommendation summary same day.
