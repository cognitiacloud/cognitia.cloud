- *Intent: one system, seven modules.* Walk modules 1–7 at headline level.

## 5. The plan — 30 / 60 / 90
- *Intent: show a credible path with a fast first win.*
- 30: foundation + first leak fixed. 60: full pipeline + AI Closer live. 90:
  visibility + optimization.
- Source: `10-roadmap-30-60-90.md`.

## 6. Pricing
- *Intent: anchor on value, make the choice easy.* Three tiers, recommend one.
- Founding-client (Client Zero) terms called out.
- Source: `11-pricing-packages.md`.

## 7. Proof — how you'll know it's working
- *Intent: replace hope with a dashboard.* Baseline → weekly/monthly metrics →
  review cadence. Honest reporting promise.
- Source: `12-proof-reporting-plan.md`.

## 8. Trust & guardrails
- *Intent: de-risk.* Cognitia control plane + proof registry; the AI discloses and
  hands off; no finance/trade-in number reaches a customer without sign-off;
  consent-first outreach.
- Source: `../internal/guardrails.md`.

## 9. What we need from you
- Decision-maker, finance/trade-in approver, inventory source, brand assets,
  channel access, 30 min/week for month one.
