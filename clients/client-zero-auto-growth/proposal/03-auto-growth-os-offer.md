## The idea in one line

Most dealerships don't have a traffic problem — they have a **leak problem**. You
already generate calls, walk-ins, and messages. The Auto Growth OS is the system
that stops the leaks and turns the demand you already have into more booked
appointments.

It is **not a website.** A website is one part. This is an operating system for
how the dealership captures, responds to, tracks, and converts demand — installed
once, improved continuously, and reported on honestly.

## What you get — 7 modules, one system

### 1. Conversion-built dealership website

A fast, mobile-first site where every vehicle has its own page, every page has a
clear next step, and inquiries are captured the moment intent appears. _(Spec:
`../playbooks/04-website-blueprint.md`.)_

### 2. Inventory that publishes itself

Your stock flows from wherever it lives today to your site and chosen channels
without manual re-entry. Each photo passes a privacy + quality check before going
live (powered by the Hermes Vision publish-safety check, so no plates, documents,
or low-quality shots slip through). _(Spec:
`../playbooks/05-inventory-automation.md`.)_

### 3. Omni-channel intake — one inbox

WhatsApp, Telegram, phone, Marketplace, and web-form inquiries land in one place
with a consistent, consent-first intake. No more "I'll check that account later."
_(Spec: `../playbooks/06-whatsapp-telegram-intake.md`.)_

### 4. AI Sales Closer

An assistant that responds in seconds — day, night, weekend — qualifies the buyer
(vehicle, timeline, budget band), recommends matching inventory, and books the
test drive. It discloses that it's an assistant, and it **hands off to a human the
instant** financing, payments, or trade-in value come up. It runs under Cognitia's
control plane, so its behavior follows rules we set with you. _(Spec:
`../playbooks/08-ai-sales-closer-script.md`.)_

### 5. CRM-lite pipeline

Every lead tracked from New → Won, with source attribution, SLA timers, and
follow-up so nothing goes cold in a spreadsheet. Designed around the tools you
already keep. _(Spec: `../playbooks/07-crm-lite-pipeline.md`.)_

### 6. Found where buyers look (SEO / AEO / GEO)

The right pages for search, structured answers for AI assistants, and a clean
local/map presence — built to best practice. We work toward visibility; we never
promise a ranking. _(Spec: `../playbooks/09-seo-aeo-geo-page-map.md`.)_

### 7. Proof you can see

A dashboard of real numbers — response time, leads captured, appointments,
attribution — reviewed with you on a set cadence. Including the weeks that are
flat. _(Spec: `../proposal/12-proof-reporting-plan.md`.)_
