## Executive Summary

This questionnaire runs during **Phase 1 (Discovery)** of the Auto Growth OS pilot. Its purpose is to (1) build an accurate picture of [Dealer]'s funnel, tools, and process; (2) **establish the baseline metrics** the pilot will be measured against; and (3) confirm **consent/compliance posture and data access** before any tooling is wired up.

Each section notes **which pilot metric or workstream the answers feed**. Use a mix of the open and structured questions below; skip what doesn't apply and add notes liberally.

> **Guardrails:** Capture **no raw PII of consumers** in this document — keep it at the aggregate/process level. Use placeholders ([Dealer], [GM Name], [Contact]) for people. Any answer indicating intent to do automated voice/SMS is flagged as **future/out-of-scope** and routed to a compliance review.

**Legend — "Feeds":** LRT = Lead Response Time · SET = Appointment Set Rate · SHOW = Show Rate · REACT = Reactivation · MERCH = Merchandising · REV = Reviews · COMPL = Compliance gate · INTEG = Integration design
