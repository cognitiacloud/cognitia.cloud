## TL;DR

Cognitia is entering a **crowded** AI-agent GTM market where the durable openings are not
technical — they are **transparent pricing, instrumented funnel measurement, and
compliance-by-design**. The right strategy is **narrow**: prove one automotive dealership
("Client Zero") end-to-end before touching a second vertical, a second rooftop, self-serve,
ads at scale, or anything token-shaped. The single biggest threat to this sprint is **scope
creep** — specifically positioning drift into a horizontal "agent OS" and a "credits" ledger
quietly mutating into a de-facto token. Both are explicitly killed/parked below.

**Recommended posture: focus, instrument, comply. Build almost nothing yet; decide and gate.**

## 2. Strongest findings (brutally honest)

1. **The market is crowded and the differentiation is commercial, not technical.** Horizontal
   AI-SDRs (11x, Artisan, Qualified, Relevance, Clay) and auto incumbents (Impel, VinSolutions,
   DealerSocket, CDK, Podium) already exist. Cognitia does **not** win on "we have agents."
   It wins on transparent pricing, measured pipeline, and compliance.
2. **The wedge is measurement.** Competitors optimize either send-volume or CRM data capture.
   Tying agent actions to an instrumented funnel (speed-to-lead → appointment set → show) is
   underserved and is also exactly what the proof harness is built to demonstrate.
3. **Compliance is the moat.** Dealer TCPA exposure is large and rising. "Compliance-by-design"
   is simultaneously a sales feature, a risk reducer, and a reason to keep voice/SMS parked.
4. **Dealership pain is execution leakage, not lead scarcity.** Speed and follow-up discipline
   compound; this is a winnable, measurable problem for a single store.
5. **A token adds risk and nothing else.** Every internal goal is met by a double-entry ledger.
   Public-token ambition is the highest-risk/lowest-value idea in scope — killed.

## 4. Decisions needed

1. Confirm **automotive dealership** as the sole near-term beachhead.
2. Confirm **flat pilot fee** as the Client Zero pricing model (park performance-share).
3. Confirm **KILL** on any public/blockchain token (internal ledger only).
4. Name the **legal/compliance sign-off owner** — required before any outreach or ad launch.
5. Confirm whether a **consenting Client Zero dealership** exists, or whether everything stays
   spec-only until one does.

## 5. Recommendation: KILL / PARK / BUILD

**KILL (stop spending any attention on these now):**
- Public token / blockchain issuance / tradable or redeemable credits.
- "Replace your sales team" messaging and any guaranteed ROI/SEO/sales claims.
- Voice/SMS automation as a near-term wedge.

**PARK (valuable later, gated, not now):**
- Multi-vertical expansion and at-scale self-serve (until Client Zero is provably repeatable).
- Performance-share pricing; paid-media management as a service; multi-rooftop rollout.
- Any externalization of credits/reports/actions (behind a mandatory legal gate).
- Mobile app lane.

**BUILD (smallest viable, only after decisions 1–4):**
- Internal double-entry credit **ledger** (centralized DB) — the simplest option.
- Goal-loop harness **MVP** (loop + 6h cadence + guardrails + scope-creep detection).
- Proof-harness **measurement scaffold** (pre-registered criteria, hold-out tasks, QC).
- Client Zero **discovery + baseline instrumentation** — *only if* a consenting dealer exists.

## 6. Scope-creep watch (the honest part)

The forbidden list exists because each item is a real temptation. Active pressure points this
sprint: (a) jumping from "spec" to "let's wire the CRM"; (b) ads readiness sliding into "just a
small test campaign"; (c) "credits" gaining transfer/redemption; (d) GTM copy drifting toward
guarantees. The artifacts encode brakes for each; the executive job is to hold them.
