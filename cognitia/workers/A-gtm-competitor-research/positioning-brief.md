## 2. Why now — the market opening (mix of VERIFIED facts + RECOMMENDED framing)

Three forces converged in 2025–2026 that create Cognitia's wedge:

1. **The AI-SDR trust gap.** The publicly reported controversy / allegations around 11x
   (reported ~$10M vs ~$3M retained, contested logos, 75–90% churn estimates, CEO out —
   reported allegations, some disputed) made buyers skeptical of *unverifiable agent
   outcomes*. **VERIFIED** that it was publicly reported (sources directly cited in
   competitor-map.md Lane C). The market now wants *proof*, not *autonomy*.
2. **Agent-identity standards are real but stop at the transaction.** ERC-8004 (on-chain
   identity + reputation, mainnet 2026-01-29), Google AP2, Visa TAP, Skyfire KYAPay all
   verify *who the agent is at payment time* — none gives an operator a durable
   *did-the-work / earned-the-outcome* ledger. **VERIFIED** (Lane A).
3. **A vertical already pays for outcome+transparency.** Numa sells pay-per-booked-
   appointment with "full AI transparency" to 1,200+ dealerships. **VERIFIED** (Lane B).
   The willingness-to-pay for proof is demonstrated, just narrowly scoped.

**RECOMMENDED conclusion:** Cognitia should NOT position as another AI agent (crowded,
distrusted). It should position as the **neutral proof/trust layer above agents** —
the thing the post-11x market is missing.

## 3. ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) — RECOMMENDED

### Beachhead ICP (Client Zero shape)
- **Who:** Independent / small-group auto dealerships (1–10 rooftops) already running or
  piloting an AI BDC/SDR/voice tool (Podium/Numa/DriveCentric/Impel).
- **Buyer:** Dealer principal / GM / BDC director — owns the number, distrusts vendor-
  reported attribution.
- **Pain:** "My AI vendor says it booked X appointments / closed Y deals. I can't verify
  it and I'm paying for it." Outcome attribution is self-reported by the vendor being paid.
- **Why they buy:** A neutral receipt for what the agent did → confidence to pay
  outcome-based, ammunition in vendor negotiations, audit trail for the GM/owner.
- **Wedge fit:** **VERIFIED** demand signal via Numa's outcome pricing; Cognitia adds
  *neutrality + cross-vendor + full funnel*.

### Expansion ICP (agent-economy / dev audience)
- **Who:** Teams building or deploying revenue-generating AI agents (vertical SaaS,
  agencies like Demandara, agent-builders) who need to *prove value to their own clients*.
- **Pain:** Selling agent outcomes into a market burned by 11x; need third-party-credible
  proof to close and retain.
- **Why they buy:** Cognitia's ledger = a trust badge / "verified by Cognitia" they can
  show *their* customers. Turns the trust deficit into a sales asset.

**RECOMMENDED sequencing:** Land in autos (Client Zero proof), expand to agencies/agent-
builders who resell trust. Demandara is the natural first expansion channel partner.

Avoid the saturated categories Cognitia *could* be slotted into:
- ❌ "AI SDR" (post-11x toxic, commoditized, >$40K/yr price war)
- ❌ "Automotive CRM" (incumbent-locked: VinSolutions/DealerSocket)
- ❌ "Agent identity / KYA" (Prove/Trulioo/Skyfire own it, capital-heavy)

**Claim a new adjacent category (RECOMMENDED):**
> **"Agent Proof & Trust Infrastructure" / "Proof-of-Outcome layer."**
> Cognitia is to AI agents what an escrow + audit + reputation bureau is to a
> marketplace: it doesn't do the work, it *proves and prices* the work.

This sidesteps every incumbent's strength and reframes their weakness (self-reported
outcomes) as the category Cognitia owns.

## 5. Three sharp value props — RECOMMENDED

### VP1 — "Neutral receipt for every agent action"
A tamper-evident action ledger that records what each AI agent did (lead touched,
appointment booked, deal advanced) independent of the vendor being paid for it.
**Proof point to build:** side-by-side Cognitia ledger vs. vendor-reported numbers for
Client Zero (synthetic fixtures only in this loop).
*Counters:* 11x trust crisis; Numa/Podium self-reported attribution.

### VP2 — "Pay for proven outcomes, not promised autonomy"
Outcome-based billing/credit logic settled against the verified ledger — extend Numa's
service-only model to the *whole funnel and across vendors*.
*Counters:* $40K–$68K/yr seat-priced AI SDRs (Qualified); flat-fee bots.

### VP3 — "Standards-native, not a walled garden"
Cognitia rides ERC-8004 reputation, AP2 mandates, and Visa TAP rather than inventing a
proprietary identity — so proofs are portable and future-proof as rails go mandatory.
**VERIFIED** substrate (Lane A). *Counters:* siloed CRMs and identity vendors that lock
proof inside their own system.

## 7. Competitive moat hypotheses — RECOMMENDED / INFERRED

  vendor being paid — they can't credibly grade their own outcomes. Cognitia's neutrality
  is a position they cannot copy without cannibalizing themselves. **INFERRED.**
- **Vertical proof data.** Client Zero gives a real auto outcome dataset → reputation
  benchmarks no horizontal trust vendor (Prove/Trulioo) has. **RECOMMENDED.**
- **Standards-native timing.** Building on ERC-8004 (live since 2026-01-29) early =
  default reputation bureau for the agent economy. **INFERRED.**
