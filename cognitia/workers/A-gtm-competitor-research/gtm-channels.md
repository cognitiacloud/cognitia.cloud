## Audience 1 — Auto dealerships (Client Zero ICP)

Ranked by hypothesized fit × cost-to-test × speed-to-signal.

### A1. Demandara warm channel (existing GTM operator) — RANK 1
- **Rationale:** Demandara already does client acquisition/fulfillment for dealers; it
  is a built-in distribution + design-partner pipeline. Lowest CAC path. **INFERRED.**
- **Cheap test (DESIGN ONLY):** Draft (do not send) a 1-page "neutral proof receipt"
  concept and a 3-question discovery script Demandara *could* use in conversations it is
  *already having*. Success metric: qualitative interest from existing relationships.
- **Guardrail:** Any actual outreach to dealers = **UNSAFE / DO NOT DO YET** (founder).

### A2. Dealer trade media + events (NADA, Digital Dealer) — RANK 2
- **Rationale:** Numa/Impel/Podium all launch at NADA; dealers trust trade press.
  **VERIFIED** that this is where the category announces (Digital Dealer / NADA coverage,
  competitor-map.md). High-credibility, content-led.
- **Cheap test (DESIGN ONLY):** Write a contributed-article draft / point-of-view piece
  ("Should you trust your AI vendor's own scoreboard?") targeting dealer trade outlets.
  Measure: would an editor accept a pitch (relationship test), not a send.
- **Guardrail:** Drafting = OK. Submitting/pitching = founder decision (not in this loop).

### A3. "Verify your AI vendor" diagnostic / lead magnet — RANK 3
- **Rationale:** Converts the trust deficit into a self-serve hook. Dealers self-assess
  whether their AI bot's reported numbers are verifiable.
- **Cheap test (DESIGN ONLY):** Build a static scorecard/checklist (10 questions:
  "Can you independently verify booked appointments? Cross-vendor? Exportable?"). Host as
  a landing page *concept* with synthetic example output. Measure design-stage: internal
  review + (later, founder-approved) organic traffic. **No paid promotion** = **UNSAFE.**

### A4. Channel/agency partnerships (BDC vendors, dealer consultants) — RANK 4
- **Rationale:** Sit *above* existing bots → partner with, not compete against, BDC
  service providers who want neutral proof to defend their own value. **INFERRED.**
- **Cheap test (DESIGN ONLY):** One-page partner brief describing the "proof layer over
  your stack" co-sell. Measure: internal viability review.

### A5. Outbound to dealer principals — RANK LAST (explicitly gated)
- **Status: UNSAFE / DO NOT DO YET.** Cold outbound to real dealers violates hard-stops
  #4 and #6. Listed only to mark it off-limits this loop. Design may be drafted; sending
  may not.

## Audience 2 — Agent-economy / developer & builder audience

Ranked by fit × cost × signal.

### B1. Open standard / open-source credibility play (ERC-8004 ecosystem) — RANK 1
- **Rationale:** ERC-8004 (mainnet 2026-01-29, backed by MetaMask/EF/Google contributors)
  is the natural home for a "reputation/proof registry." Building visibly on it earns
  dev trust and SEO/topical authority. **VERIFIED** substrate (competitor-map.md Lane A).

- **Guardrail:** No token launch, no on-chain mainnet deploy with real value. Spec +
  sandbox only. **UNSAFE** beyond that.

  **INFERRED.**

  vs. enterprise governance tools). Measure at design stage: keyword opportunity review
  (no rankings guaranteed — **UNSAFE** to promise SEO outcomes per hard-stop #9).

### B4. Community presence (agent/AI builder communities, hackathons) — RANK 4

  community-driven. **INFERRED.**
- **Cheap test (DESIGN ONLY):** Draft a hackathon-bounty concept ("build a proof receipt

### B5. Paid acquisition (ads) — RANK LAST (gated)
- **Status: UNSAFE / DO NOT DO YET.** Any paid ad launch violates hard-stop #5. Design of
  ad *concepts* permitted for later founder review; launching is not.

## Cross-cutting test-design principles (RECOMMENDED)

1. **Every "test" here is a design, executable only after founder sign-off.** This loop
   produces the design, never the send/launch.
2. **Lead with proof, not autonomy** — every channel message concept frames the
   post-11x trust gap (the one durable, defensible hook). **RECOMMENDED.**
3. **Two-front message discipline:** dealers hear "neutral receipt for your AI vendor";
   builders hear "standards-native proof-of-outcome layer." Same product, two framings.
4. **No guaranteed-outcome language anywhere** (no ROI, lead volume, SEO rank, close
   rate) — hard-stop #9. **UNSAFE** to promise.
