## Brief 1 — "The Inspection You Can See" (Trust / CPO)

- **Concept:** Walk through what "certified pre-owned" actually means at Sample Auto Group — the
  inspection, the reconditioning, the warranty — to earn trust before the visit.
- **Angle:** Transparency beats hype. Show the checklist, not a salesperson.
- **Format:** 9:16 short-form video, <15s.
- **Script outline:**
  1. Hook (0-2s): "Wondering what 'certified' actually means?"
  2. Body (2-10s): Quick cuts of a multi-point inspection; on-screen "multi-point inspection."
  3. Offer (10-13s): "CPO Event this weekend at Sample Auto Group."
  4. CTA (13-15s): "Book a test drive — link below." (no payment claim)
- **Compliance notes:**
  - The inspection claim must be **substantiated** — only state the inspection scope the dealer
    actually performs (FTC: back up your claims). VERIFIED basis below.
  - "Certified pre-owned" is fine if the program genuinely certifies.
  - **UNSAFE:** "Guaranteed like-new condition." → **Reframe:** "Reconditioned and inspected;
    see the inspection report on each vehicle."
  - No financing terms stated -> no TILA trigger here. Keep it that way.

## Brief 2 — "Payments, Up Front" (Payment framing — TILA-sensitive)

- **Concept:** Let shoppers see an *estimated* monthly payment range before visiting.
- **Angle:** Reduce friction/anxiety by being upfront about cost.
- **Format:** Static single image + carousel of inventory.
- **Script/copy outline:**
  - Headline: "See estimated payments before you visit."
  - Subhead: "CPO Event — Sample Auto Group."
  - CTA: "Estimate yours."
- **Compliance notes (HIGH RISK — this is the financing landmine):**
  - **UNSAFE:** "$199/mo!" with no other terms. Stating a payment amount is a **Reg Z trigger
    term** and forces disclosure of down payment, repayment terms, and **APR** in close proximity.
    (VERIFIED basis below.)
    **Reframe (compliant):** If a payment figure is shown, add a clear-and-conspicuous block:
    "Estimated $___/mo. Example: $___ financed for __ months at __% APR; $___ due at signing.
    On approved credit. Your terms may vary." — OR avoid the number entirely and say "Estimate
    your payment in 60 seconds."
  - **UNSAFE:** "Guaranteed financing approval" / "Everyone approved." → **Reframe:**
    "Financing available on approved credit. Apply to see if you qualify." (FTC explicitly treats
    "guaranteed approval / pre-approval" representations as deceptive risk.)
  - **UNSAFE:** "0% APR for everyone." → **Reframe:** "0% APR available to qualified buyers on
    select models; not all buyers will qualify."
  - Best practice: advertising **APR alone** does not trigger extra Reg Z disclosures; advertising
    a **payment/down-payment amount** does. Prefer APR-only or no-number framing. (VERIFIED basis.)

## Brief 3 — "This Weekend Only" (Scarcity / Event)

- **Concept:** Drive event-weekend urgency for the CPO Event.
- **Angle:** Genuine time-bound event, real end date.
- **Format:** 9:16 short-form video, <12s.
- **Script outline:**
  1. Hook (0-2s): "The CPO Event ends Sunday."
  2. Body (2-8s): Inventory b-roll; "select certified pre-owned vehicles."
  3. CTA (8-12s): "Visit this weekend — book your test drive."
- **Compliance notes:**
  - **UNSAFE:** A perpetual/looping "ends soon!" countdown that never actually ends (deceptive
    urgency). → **Reframe:** Only run scarcity copy when the event genuinely ends on the stated
    date; pull the creative when it does.
  - **UNSAFE:** "Save up to $10,000 on every vehicle!" → **Reframe:** "Savings up to $___ on
    select vehicles; offer varies by model. See dealer for details." ("Up to" requires the top
    figure to be genuinely available on a meaningful number of units.)
  - **UNSAFE:** Advertising a specific CPO unit at a price you don't actually have available
    (bait-and-switch). → **Reframe:** Only advertise specific units/prices that are in stock and
    honored.
