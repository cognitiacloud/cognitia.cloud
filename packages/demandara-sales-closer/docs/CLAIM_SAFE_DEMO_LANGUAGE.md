# V2-8 Claim-Safe Demo Language

## Internal-safe
"We have a local mock product-spine demo for Demandara Sales Closer. It runs fake Budget Wheels fixtures through intake, qualification, consent, human approval, mock writeback, proof receipt, and static console trace. It is not shipped or production-ready."

## Investor-safe
"We can demonstrate an early local prototype of a governed GTM workflow. It shows the intended control architecture and proof trail using fake data only. It does not demonstrate live customer traction, revenue, production deployment, live CRM integration, or autonomous outreach."

## Customer-safe
"This is a demo using fake data to show how a future governed lead workflow could work. It does not access your CRM, contact customers, send messages, or make automated decisions in production."

## What this proves
- The product spine can run end-to-end locally.
- Consent and approval gates can block unsafe paths.
- Mock writeback and proof receipts can be produced deterministically.
- Operator proof review can be shown without live systems.

## What this does not prove
- Live CRM readiness.
- Live outreach readiness.
- Production security.
- Real identity/signature approval readiness.
- Revenue, customer adoption, or Alta parity.
