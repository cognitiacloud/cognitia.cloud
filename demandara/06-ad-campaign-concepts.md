# 10 Ad Campaign Concepts

> **CONCEPTS ONLY.** Nothing here is launched and **no budget is authorized**.
> These are blueprints a human Reviewer can later take through the relevant
> readiness checklist ([01](01-google-ads-readiness.md) /
> [02](02-meta-ads-readiness.md) / [03](03-tiktok-readiness.md)) and the launch
> gate in [00](00-engine-overview.md). Audiences are described as **themes**, not
> prohibited targeting. No financing promises.

### Concept 1 — "New Arrivals" Vehicle Ads (Google)
- **Platform/objective:** Google Vehicle Ads (VLA) / Shopping — sales-intent capture.
- **Audience theme:** in-market shoppers searching make/model/`{{CITY}}` near you.
- **Funnel:** bottom (high intent).
- **Creative angle:** real VIN photos, fee-inclusive price, "available now."
- **Offer framing:** availability + transparency, **not** payment.
- **Success metric:** VDP views, `lead_submit`, `call_click`, store visits.
- **Compliance:** honest fee-inclusive pricing; disclaimers from [08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md).

### Concept 2 — Branded Search Defense (Google)
- **Platform/objective:** Search — capture people already searching `{{DEALER_NAME}}`.
- **Audience theme:** brand + "near me" queries.
- **Funnel:** bottom.
- **Creative angle:** hours, directions, inventory, "no-pressure test drive."
- **Success metric:** CTR, `directions_click`, calls.
- **Compliance:** trademark policy; no superlatives ([09](09-what-not-to-claim.md)).

### Concept 3 — Local Inventory Awareness (Meta)
- **Platform/objective:** Meta Advantage+ / awareness — show the lot's range.
- **Audience theme:** `{{CITY}}` + 15-mile radius general local reach.
- **Funnel:** top.
- **Creative angle:** carousel of body styles; "your `{{CITY}}` lot."
- **Success metric:** reach, `ViewContent`, profile/site visits.
- **Compliance:** if any financing message attaches → **Credit Special Ad
  Category** + restricted targeting ([02](02-meta-ads-readiness.md)).

### Concept 4 — Service & Maintenance Retention (Meta)
- **Platform/objective:** Meta — drive service bookings.
- **Audience theme:** consented past-customer email audience (hashed) + local.
- **Funnel:** retention.
- **Creative angle:** "keep it running right" seasonal service.
- **Offer framing:** service value, **no** payment/credit.
- **Success metric:** `Lead`, `Contact`, booking form submits.
- **Compliance:** consent-documented audience; no price guarantees.

### Concept 5 — Short-Form Brand Builder (TikTok)
- **Platform/objective:** TikTok — top-funnel reach via native reels.
- **Audience theme:** broad `{{CITY}}` interest, let the algorithm find fit.
- **Funnel:** top.
- **Creative angle:** reels from [05](05-reel-ideas.md) (`#walkaround`, `#bts`).
- **Success metric:** views, 6s view rate, profile visits, site clicks.
- **Compliance:** **vehicles not financing**; Commercial Music Library;
  no absolute claims ([03](03-tiktok-readiness.md)).

### Concept 6 — VDP Retargeting (Meta, consent-safe)
- **Platform/objective:** Meta — re-engage people who viewed a vehicle.
- **Audience theme:** **consented** site visitors via Pixel+CAPI; deduped.
- **Funnel:** middle/bottom.
- **Creative angle:** "still thinking about the `{{MODEL}}`? It's still here."
- **Success metric:** return VDP views, `Lead`.
- **Compliance:** retarget only consented users; `ad_personalization` honored;
  no PII when consent denied ([07](07-landing-page-tracking-plan.md)).

### Concept 7 — Trade-In Lead Gen (Google or Meta)
- **Platform/objective:** lead gen — "value your trade."
- **Audience theme:** in-market upgraders; local.
- **Funnel:** middle.
- **Creative angle:** "see what your car could be worth" → appraisal form.
- **Offer framing:** appraisal, **no value or payoff guarantees**.
- **Success metric:** appraisal-form `lead_submit`.
- **Compliance:** no guaranteed trade value ([09](09-what-not-to-claim.md)).

### Concept 8 — Model-Year Changeover (Google + Meta)
- **Platform/objective:** seasonal sales push on outgoing-year units.
- **Audience theme:** in-market for that segment; local radius.
- **Funnel:** bottom.
- **Creative angle:** "outgoing-year selection" + availability.
- **Offer framing:** selection/availability, fee-inclusive pricing where shown.
- **Success metric:** VDP views, leads, calls.
- **Compliance:** honest pricing; "while supplies last" disclaimer ([08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md)).

### Concept 9 — Community / Event Awareness (Meta + GBP)
- **Platform/objective:** local goodwill + foot traffic for a dealer event.
- **Audience theme:** `{{CITY}}` community, families, local interests.
- **Funnel:** top.
- **Creative angle:** event reel + GBP Post; "join us at `{{DEALER_NAME}}`."
- **Success metric:** event responses, `directions_click`, GBP actions.
- **Compliance:** no financing tie-in; honest event terms.

### Concept 10 — Reviews & Reputation (Meta + GBP)
- **Platform/objective:** trust-building social proof.
- **Audience theme:** local awareness + consented past customers.
- **Funnel:** middle (trust).
- **Creative angle:** animated verified reviews (reel #18), "real `{{CITY}}` drivers."
- **Success metric:** engagement, profile visits, review volume lift.
- **Compliance:** testimonial disclosure ([08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md));
  no incentivized/fake reviews ([09](09-what-not-to-claim.md)).
