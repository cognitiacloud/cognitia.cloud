# What NOT to Claim in Ads

The red-flag list for `{{DEALER_NAME}}`. Every ad, reel, caption, and landing
page passes this review **before** it reaches a readiness checklist. Each entry:
the **banned phrase**, **why** (FTC / Reg Z / platform), and a **compliant
rewrite**.

> Rule of thumb: if a claim promises an outcome you can't guarantee for every
> reader (approval, price, savings, value), it's a red flag. Vehicles and
> service: fine to celebrate. Credit and money: handle with extreme care and
> the disclaimers in [08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md).

## 1. Credit & financing guarantees — the #1 risk

| ❌ Don't say | Why | ✅ Instead |
|-------------|-----|-----------|
| "Guaranteed approval" / "Everyone's approved" | False promise of credit; FTC + platform violation; impossible to honor | "Financing options for many credit situations, on approved credit" |
| "No credit? No problem — you're approved!" | Implies guaranteed credit | "Ask about financing options — apply to see what you qualify for" |
| "$0 down guaranteed" | Unqualified payment guarantee | "Low- and no-down-payment options may be available, OAC" |
| "Bad credit? Drive today!" | Targets/implies financial status; guarantee | "We work with a range of lenders. Subject to approval." |
| "We'll get you approved" | Promise of approval | "We'll help you apply; approval is up to the lender" |

**Why it matters most:** hard rule #3 forbids credit/financing promises. These
also trip Meta's Credit Special Ad Category, TikTok's financial-services rules,
and FTC deception standards. **Any** financing statement needs
[08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md) blocks B/D and **Counsel sign-off**.

## 2. Reg Z "trigger term" traps

| ❌ Don't say (alone) | Why | ✅ Instead |
|---------------------|-----|-----------|
| "0% APR!" with no terms | APR is a trigger term → requires full disclosures | "0% APR available on approved credit — see financing example + terms" |
| "$199/mo!" with no terms | Payment amount triggers Reg Z | "$199/mo example, OAC — full terms below" |
| "$0 down, 72 months" | Down payment + term trigger Reg Z | Include APR, repayment terms, down payment per [08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md) D |
| "Low monthly payments" + a number | Triggers disclosures if specific | Keep general OR add full Reg Z disclosure |

**Fix:** if you state a rate, payment, down payment, term, or finance charge, you
**must** disclose down payment, repayment terms, and APR ([08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md) D).

## 3. Pricing & "deal" claims

| ❌ Don't say | Why | ✅ Instead |
|-------------|-----|-----------|
| Low price that hides doc/prep/shipping fees | Google dishonest-pricing policy; FTC bait | Advertise the **all-in** price ([08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md) A) |
| "Lowest price in `{{STATE}}`" / "Beat any deal" | Unsubstantiated superlative | "Competitive pricing — see our current offers" |
| "Was $30,000, now $22,000" (fake original) | Fictitious price comparison | Use a real, substantiated basis ([08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md) E) |
| "Free car!" / "Win a car" (when not truly free) | Deceptive | Only if genuinely free with terms disclosed |
| Price for a stock photo, not the real VIN | Bait-and-switch | Real VIN, real photo, real price ([08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md) C) |

## 4. Fake urgency & scarcity

| ❌ Don't say | Why | ✅ Instead |
|-------------|-----|-----------|
| Evergreen "ends tonight!" countdown that never ends | Deceptive urgency | Real `{{END_DATE}}` ([08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md) F) |
| "Only 1 left!" when untrue | False scarcity | "Limited availability — subject to prior sale" |

## 5. Discrimination & prohibited targeting

| ❌ Don't do | Why | ✅ Instead |
|------------|-----|-----------|
| Target/exclude by age, gender, ZIP, or "financial status" for credit ads | Meta Credit Special Ad Category + fair-lending/anti-discrimination law | Use the restricted Special Ad Category settings ([02](02-meta-ads-readiness.md)) |
| Copy like "perfect for young families only" used to exclude | Proxy discrimination | Describe the **vehicle**, not the protected audience |
| "Struggling with bad credit?" personal-attribute framing | Implies you know the user's finances (Meta personal-attributes policy) | "Financing options available — apply to learn more" |

## 6. Testimonials, reviews & endorsements

| ❌ Don't do | Why | ✅ Instead |
|------------|-----|-----------|
| Fake/incentivized reviews without disclosure | FTC endorsement rules | Real reviews + disclosure ([08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md) G) |
| "Typical customer saves $5,000" | Unsubstantiated results claim | "Individual experiences vary" |
| Use a customer's face without consent | Right of publicity / privacy | Signed media release on file |

## 7. Product & safety claims

| ❌ Don't say | Why | ✅ Instead |
|-------------|-----|-----------|
| "Safest car ever" / "Zero accidents" | Unsubstantiated absolute | "Equipped with `{{FEATURE}}` driver-assist — see specs" |
| "Self-driving" for driver-assist | Misrepresents capability | "Advanced driver-assistance features (hands-on required)" |
| Inflated MPG/range vs EPA | Deceptive | Cite EPA estimates as published |
| Stunt/speeding footage as normal use | Unsafe + policy risk | Lawful, safe driving footage only |

## 8. Platform-specific absolutes

| ❌ Don't say | Platform | ✅ Instead |
|-------------|----------|-----------|
| "Get approved in 10 seconds" | TikTok bans absolute time/finance claims | "Apply online — it's quick" (no guarantee) |
| Loan-calculator / payment-graphic imagery (unintended) | Can auto-trigger Meta Credit category | Use vehicle imagery unless Credit category is intended/handled |
| Unlicensed popular music on a business account | Copyright / platform policy | Commercial Music Library ([03](03-tiktok-readiness.md), [05](05-reel-ideas.md)) |

## 9. Quick "stop words" scan

Flag any of these for review before publishing:

```
guaranteed · approved · everyone qualifies · no credit needed · $0 down (alone)
lowest · cheapest · best price · beat any deal · #1 · unbeatable
APR/%/mo/payment/down/term (without Reg Z disclosures)
ends tonight (if recurring) · only 1 left (if untrue)
self-driving · safest · zero risk · free (if not truly free)
bad credit · struggling · poor credit (personal-attribute framing)
```
