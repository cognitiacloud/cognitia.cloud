# 20 Reel Ideas (Short-Form Vehicle Content)

Reusable short-form concepts for `{{DEALER_NAME}}`. All are **finance-free by
design** — no price/payment/APR/approval claims in hooks or captions. Use
licensed audio only (TikTok Commercial Music Library for business use). Each
idea has a reusable **template tag** so you can rerun it with new inventory.

**Format conventions:** 9:16 vertical, hook in the first 2 seconds, on-screen
captions always on, CTA points to `{{URL}}` with UTMs (see
[07](07-landing-page-tracking-plan.md)). Run every script through
[09](09-what-not-to-claim.md) before posting.

---

### 1. "3 reasons this one sells fast" — `#feature-rundown`
- **Hook:** "Three reasons this `{{MODEL}}` won't last the weekend."
- **Beats:** quick cuts on 3 standout features → exterior glamour shot → CTA.
- **On-screen:** numbered 1/2/3 + feature names. **CTA:** "See it on our lot."

### 2. New-arrival reveal — `#new-arrival`
- **Hook:** "Just landed in `{{CITY}}`."
- **Beats:** transporter/cover-pull tease → 360 walk → badge close-up.
- **CTA:** "DM 'INFO' for details." (no price on screen)

### 3. New vs Certified Pre-Owned — `#education`
- **Hook:** "New or certified pre-owned — which is actually for you?"
- **Beats:** split-screen pros/cons, neutral and factual. **No payment math.**
- **CTA:** "Tap to browse both."

### 4. 60-second walkaround — `#walkaround`
- **Hook:** "Full tour in 60 seconds."
- **Beats:** exterior → interior → cargo → tech → start-up sound.
- **CTA:** "Book a test drive."

### 5. Hidden features you missed — `#feature-rundown`
- **Hook:** "Bet you didn't know your `{{MODEL}}` does this."
- **Beats:** 3–4 lesser-known features demoed. **CTA:** "More tips on our page."

### 6. Test-drive POV — `#pov`
- **Hook:** "POV: your first drive in the `{{MODEL}}`."
- **Beats:** handheld/gimbal seat POV, ambient sound, no speeding/unsafe driving.
- **CTA:** "Reserve a test drive."

### 7. Meet the team — `#people`
- **Hook:** "The person who'll actually help you — not a chatbot."
- **Beats:** staffer intro, one fun fact, "ask for me." **CTA:** "Come say hi."

### 8. Service bay BTS — `#bts`
- **Hook:** "What really happens before a car hits our lot."
- **Beats:** inspection/detailing montage. Builds trust. **CTA:** "Shop inspected inventory."

### 9. Delivery day — `#social-proof`
- **Hook:** "Another happy `{{CITY}}` driver."
- **Beats:** keys handoff, smiles (with written consent). **CTA:** "Your turn?"
- **Note:** get a signed media release; see [08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md).

### 10. "This or that" — `#engagement`
- **Hook:** "Truck or SUV — pick one."
- **Beats:** two vehicles, quick compare, ask viewers to comment.
- **CTA:** "Comment your pick."

### 11. 5 used-car checks — `#education`
- **Hook:** "5 things to check before buying ANY used car."
- **Beats:** tires, fluids, history report, test drive, inspection. Genuinely useful.
- **CTA:** "We do all 5 for you."

### 12. Cargo/space test — `#demo`
- **Hook:** "Will it fit? Let's find out."
- **Beats:** load luggage/stroller/golf clubs/dog crate. **CTA:** "Need the space? Test it."

### 13. Tech deep-dive — `#feature-rundown`
- **Hook:** "Your car's smartest feature, explained."
- **Beats:** infotainment/driver-assist demo, clear narration. **CTA:** "See it live."

### 14. Seasonal prep — `#seasonal`
- **Hook:** "Getting `{{CITY}}` ready for winter."
- **Beats:** AWD, remote start, all-weather mats. **CTA:** "Shop winter-ready."

### 15. Myth-busting — `#education`
- **Hook:** "Dealership myth: ___. Here's the truth."
- **Beats:** bust a common myth honestly (e.g., "test drives are pressure-free here").
- **CTA:** "No-pressure test drive."

### 16. Trade-in explainer — `#education`
- **Hook:** "How a trade-in appraisal actually works."
- **Beats:** walk the process; **no value/payment guarantees**. **CTA:** "Get yours appraised."

### 17. Lot tour montage — `#inventory`
- **Hook:** "30 seconds, 10 cars, one lot."
- **Beats:** fast cuts across inventory variety. **CTA:** "Browse the full lot."

### 18. Customer review on screen — `#social-proof`
- **Hook:** "Real review from a real `{{CITY}}` customer."
- **Beats:** animate a verified 5-star review; cite source. **CTA:** "Read more reviews."
- **Note:** testimonials need the disclosure in [08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md).

### 19. "A day at `{{DEALER_NAME}}`" — `#bts`
- **Hook:** "What a day here looks like."
- **Beats:** open-to-close time-lapse, team, deliveries. **CTA:** "Visit us this week."

### 20. FAQ rapid-fire — `#education`
- **Hook:** "Your top 3 questions, answered fast."
- **Beats:** 3 real FAQs (hours, test drives, inspection) — **avoid financing**.
- **CTA:** "More questions? DM us."

## Reusable template tags (reuse engine)

| Tag | Reuse pattern |
|-----|---------------|
| `#feature-rundown` | Swap vehicle → same 3-feature structure |
| `#new-arrival` | New unit → reveal format |
| `#walkaround` | Any VIN → 60s tour |
| `#education` | Evergreen, no inventory dependency |
| `#social-proof` | New testimonial → same frame |
| `#bts` / `#people` | Rotate staff/departments |
| `#seasonal` | Swap season + relevant features |
| `#engagement` | Rotate the "this or that" pairing |

## Compliance reminders for every reel

- No price, payment, APR, "$0 down", or "approval" claims in hooks/captions.
- Licensed audio only; no unlicensed popular tracks for business accounts.
- Get written media releases for any identifiable customer
  ([08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md)).
- Testimonials reflect individual experience — add the disclosure.
- Drive safely on camera; no stunts, speeding, or distracted-driving footage.
