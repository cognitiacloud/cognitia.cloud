# TikTok Readiness Checklist

For `{{DEALER_NAME}}`. TikTok rewards native, fast, authentic short-form — but
its **financial-services rules are strict and gated**. Get the account, tracking,
and creative ready — then stop at the launch gate. **This checklist does not
launch anything and authorizes no spend.**

> **2026 policy headline:** Automotive *sales* content is generally allowed, but
> **financing/credit is a restricted financial-services category**. It typically
> requires going through TikTok's **Industry Entry** process with licensing
> documentation, is usually **not available via self-serve approval**, and bans
> "absolute" claims (e.g., "get money in 10 seconds"), guaranteed returns, and
> risk-minimizing language. **Default plan: promote vehicles, not financing.**

## 1. Account & access prerequisites

- [ ] TikTok account for `{{DEALER_NAME}}` (organic presence established first).
- [ ] **TikTok Business Account** + **TikTok Ads Manager** account created.
- [ ] **TikTok Business Center** set up; assets and roles assigned.
- [ ] Ad account in **USD**, correct time zone; **no budget / paused** (rule #1).
- [ ] Operator has advertiser access; Reviewer holds admin/finance.

## 2. Eligibility & verification

- [ ] Business verification completed in Business Center.
- [ ] **Industry / category eligibility checked.** If any financing message is
      involved: prepare for **Industry Entry** (licensing + regulatory docs);
      assume **no self-serve** approval. Until approved, **do not** run finance
      creative.
- [ ] Confirm automotive *sales* creative (no financing) is eligible for
      standard approval in the US market.

## 3. Tracking & conversions (consent-safe)

- [ ] **TikTok Pixel** and/or **Events API** installed on `{{URL}}`.
- [ ] Consent gating in place via the CMP (see [07](07-landing-page-tracking-plan.md));
      no events fire before consent where required.
- [ ] Events mapped: `ViewContent` (VDP), `SubmitForm` (lead), `Contact`
      (call), `ClickButton` (CTA).
- [ ] Advanced Matching sends **hashed** data only, consent-respecting.
- [ ] Validated in TikTok Events Manager test mode.

## 4. Policy eligibility (review before building)

- [ ] **No absolute claims** about time/region/brand ("instant approval",
      "lowest price anywhere") — TikTok deceptive-practices policy.
- [ ] **No financing guarantees**, no "get approved fast", no risk-minimizing
      finance language. Route any finance copy to Counsel **and** Industry Entry.
- [ ] Cross-check [09](09-what-not-to-claim.md) for banned language.
- [ ] Required disclaimers from [08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md) present where
      price/financing appears (and legible at mobile speed).
- [ ] Music: use **TikTok's Commercial Music Library** for ads/business use — do
      not promise or use unlicensed popular tracks (see [05](05-reel-ideas.md)).
- [ ] Branded Content / disclosure toggle used correctly for any paid creator
      partnership.

## 5. Creative spec sheet

| Item | Spec | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Video | 9:16, ≥720p, 9–34s sweet spot | Native, hook in first 2s |
| Captions | On-screen text | Auto-captions + key claims as text |
| Audio | Commercial Music Library | No unlicensed tracks for business use |
| CTA | "Learn more" / "Shop now" | Points to `{{URL}}` with UTMs |
| Disclaimers | Persistent on-screen | Price/fees/OAC must remain legible |

## 6. Short-form content fit (why TikTok is different)

- Lead with the vehicle and the human, not the offer.
- Education + entertainment outperform hard-sell; save offers for the CTA.
- Keep claims behavior-safe: feature walkarounds, tips, BTS — **not** payment math.
- Reuse the reel concepts in [05](05-reel-ideas.md); they're built finance-free.

## 7. Pre-flight checklist (final, before the gate)

- [ ] Sections 1–6 complete.
- [ ] Finance creative is **excluded** unless Industry Entry is approved.
- [ ] Pixel/Events API validated; consent honored in test mode.
- [ ] No campaign published; no budget set.
