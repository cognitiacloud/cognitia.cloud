# Dealership Content Calendar

A reusable organic cadence for `{{DEALER_NAME}}`, a mixed-inventory (new + used)
dealer in `{{CITY}}, {{STATE}}`. Organic content carries **no spend** and makes
**no financing promises**. It feeds the reels in [05](05-reel-ideas.md) and warms
audiences for the (human-launched) paid concepts in [06](06-ad-campaign-concepts.md).

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Inventory** | Show the metal | New arrivals, feature walkarounds |
| **Education** | Build trust | Buying tips, maintenance, "new vs used" |
| **People & BTS** | Humanize | Staff intros, service bay, delivery day |
| **Social proof** | Reduce risk | Testimonials, reviews, delivery photos |
| **Community / Local** | Local SEO + goodwill | Events, sponsorships, `{{CITY}}` shoutouts |
| **Service & ownership** | Retain | Service specials (no price promises), care tips |

> Keep financing **out** of organic. Talk vehicles, people, and service. Route
> any payment/credit topic through [08](08-compliance-disclaimers.md) +
> [09](09-what-not-to-claim.md), and only in approved paid placements.

## GBP / Local SEO weekly task lane

Run this every week regardless of paid activity — it compounds.

- [ ] **2–3 GBP Posts** (offers/events/updates) — no financing promises.
- [ ] **Upload 5–10 fresh photos** (lot, new arrivals, team, delivered cars).
- [ ] **Respond to every review** within 24–48h (positive and negative).
- [ ] **Answer new GBP Q&A**; seed 2–3 common questions.
- [ ] **Verify NAP** consistency on site + top citations once/month.
- [ ] **Check categories/attributes** are current (financing-available attribute
      only if accurate; no promotional claims).
- [ ] **Track** which GBP actions (calls, directions, site clicks) move — feeds
      [07](07-landing-page-tracking-plan.md).
