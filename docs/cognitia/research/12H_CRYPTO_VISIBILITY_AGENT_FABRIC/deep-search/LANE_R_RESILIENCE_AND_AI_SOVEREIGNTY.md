# LANE R — Resilience & AI Sovereignty (sober thesis)

**Objective**: Verify whether "AI compute restriction / shutdown" pressures are
real, then convert them into a *sober* resilience thesis — NOT an evasion pitch.

**Sources (WebSearch)**: Greenberg Traurig (GPU export controls 2025); Introl,
DataCenterKnowledge, Congress.gov CRS R48642, CSIS, Contrary Research, eeNews
(Remote Access Security Act). 

## Verified-enough facts (public policy, multi-source)
- `verified_fact` (reported) — The US introduced sweeping AI-chip/GPU export
  controls (the Jan 2025 "AI Diffusion Rule"), modified through 2025; advanced
  GPUs (H100/H200/Blackwell) are restricted to certain tiers/countries.
- `verified_fact` (reported) — The **Remote Access Security Act (H.R. 2683)**
  passed the US House on 12 Jan 2026 (369–22), extending export-control logic to
  *remote/cloud* access of controlled compute.
- `verified_fact` (reported) — Nations are pursuing semiconductor self-sufficiency
  (e.g. China 15%→25%, 2019→2025).

## The sober resilience thesis (what this means for Cognitia)
- `likely_inference` — Access to frontier *cloud* compute is becoming more
  policy-contingent and geographically constrained. Organizations increasingly
  value **portability** and **user-owned / self-hosted compute** as a continuity
  measure — not to evade law, but to reduce single-vendor / single-jurisdiction
  dependency.
- This is the legitimate motivation for Cognitia's distributed agent fabric
  (LOOP 5): let agent work run across a user's own machines (Mac/Win/Linux/cloud),
  route to local OR cloud models, and carry **portable, verifiable proof** of work
  regardless of where it ran.

## Relevance to Cognitia
Resilience = "your agent work and its proofs are portable across compute you
control." That is a *continuity and sovereignty* value proposition, fully
compatible with operating lawfully in every jurisdiction.

## Gaps
- The fabric is design-only; no resilience claim is demonstrable yet.

## Recommended actions
- Frame the fabric as "resilient, user-owned, portable, verifiable" — see LOOP 5
  `RISKS_AND_FALSE_CLAIMS.md` for the banned framings.

## Public-safe wording
"Cognitia researches a resilient agent fabric: user-owned compute, local-or-cloud
routing, and portable, verifiable proof of work — to reduce single-vendor and
single-jurisdiction dependency."

## Unsafe claims to avoid (HARD)
No "impossible to shut down," "censorship-proof," "evade government/sanctions/
export controls," "unregulated AI," "operate where it's banned." Resilience ≠
evasion. Never position the fabric as a way to break the law.
