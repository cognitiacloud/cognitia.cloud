# Claims We Do Not Make

Honest self-limitation. Cognitia deliberately refuses the following claims; if you
ever see Cognitia (or someone speaking for it) make one of these, treat it as
unauthorized.

## Product / operational

- **Not production-ready.** Cognitia is not production-deployed and makes no
  production-readiness claim.
- **Not SOC 2 certified.** No SOC 2 (or other) certification is claimed.
- **No external security audit yet.** No "audited" claim until a real audit with a
  published report exists.
- **Managed-Postgres RLS not yet proven.** Engine-level row-level security under a
  restricted (non-superuser) role on a managed database is not yet verified.

## Architecture / resilience

- **Not decentralized in production.** No claim of production decentralization.
- **Not uncensorable.** No "censorship-proof" claim.
- **Not "impossible to shut down" / not "unstoppable."** Any resilience work is
  about continuity and user-owned compute, never evasion.

## Token / financial

- **No token sale.** No public token, presale, public sale, or purchase path.
- **No investment return.** No price targets, expected/guaranteed returns, APY,
  yield, or passive income.
- **No mainnet.** No mainnet deployment or contract.
- **No real payments / token transfers** today; internal credits only.
- **No exchange listing**; no "get in early"; no "next Ethereum/Solana."

## Evidence integrity

- **No fabricated data** — no fake on-chain metrics, holders, liquidity, TVL,
  community counts, partnerships, customers, revenue, or pilots.
- **No invented quotes, statistics, or transcripts.**

## Why publish this

Naming the limits is itself a trust signal: a researcher can hold Cognitia to this
list. The corresponding _positive_, reproducible evidence is in
`RESEARCHER_PACK.md` and `VERIFY_IT_YOURSELF.md`.
