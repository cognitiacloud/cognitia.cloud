# Unit Economics Model — Client Zero Pilot

> **Illustrative internal model.** All figures are planning assumptions, not a
> forecast, quote, or guarantee. Edit the assumption tables to fit reality
> before quoting. Pairs with
> [`client-zero-pilot-offer.md`](./client-zero-pilot-offer.md).

## Purpose

Size the cost and rough margin of running one dealership through the
**Demandara Sales Closer** proof-governed lead-to-appointment pilot, under the
**Cognitia** consent + human-approval controls, so pricing decisions are made on
honest numbers. "Client Zero" here is the internal label for the first paid
pilot dealership.

## What's being priced

The core, recurring deliverable is the **proof-governed operating model** plus a
**monthly proof reporting deliverable** (subject to signed pilot scope). The
proof layer is run as a **pilot control model / sandbox-demo proof workflow**
during the pilot — not billed as finished production proof infrastructure.
Hermes Vision content/QC is an **optional add-on**, priced separately.

## Human-in-the-loop time model (editable)

The governance model is human-gated, so labor is the main cost. Assumptions:

| Activity | Est. minutes / month |
|----------|----------------------|
| Lead-handling review & exception handling | 240 |
| Consent-gate review | 60 |
| Human approval of outbound actions | 180 |
| Dealer comms / coordination | 120 |
| Proof report assembly | 120 |
| **Total** | **~720 min (~12 hrs)** |

| Assumption | Value (CAD) |
|------------|-------------|
| Loaded hourly cost | $40 / hr |
| Monthly human-labor cost (12 hrs) | **~$480** |

Adjust hours and rate per tier — Starter trends lower, Managed Growth higher.

## Estimated cost & margin (illustrative)

Using ~$480 labor + ~$150 tooling/overhead ≈ **~$630/mo** baseline cost for a
moderate-volume dealer:

| Tier | Monthly (CAD) | Est. cost | Est. gross margin |
|------|---------------|-----------|-------------------|
| Starter Pilot | $500–$750 | ~$400–$500 | thin; volume-controlled |
| Recommended Pilot | $1,000–$1,500 | ~$600–$700 | healthier |
| Managed Growth Pilot | $1,500–$2,500 | ~$800–$1,100 | best, more labor |

All figures are estimates for planning only.

## Sensitivity notes

Margin erodes with:

- Scope creep beyond fixed lead-handling volume.
- Excess approvals / exception handling.
- Content add-on volume above the agreed cap.

Offer controls that protect margin:

- Fixed lead-handling volume per tier.
- Revision caps on the optional content add-on.
- Content/QC add-on priced separately, not bundled into base labor.

## Ads handling

Ads are **out of scope** for the pilot economics. If ads are added later by
separate written agreement, **ad spend is paid directly by the dealer and is
never fronted by Cognitia** — it does not appear on Cognitia's books and is
excluded from these numbers.
