# Dealer Discovery Script — Client Zero Pilot

> **Internal prep document. Not a contract, not legal advice, and not an
> authorization to do outreach.** Use it to run a qualifying conversation with a
> Canadian dealer for the **Demandara Sales Closer** proof-governed
> lead-to-appointment pilot, governed by **Cognitia** controls. "Client Zero" is
> our internal label for the first paid pilot dealership.

## How to use / guardrails

- This script **does not authorize live outreach.** Founder/counsel sign-off is
  required before any live outreach (see checklist).
- **No positive promises.** Do not say "guaranteed leads," "we'll increase your
  sales," a specific number of leads, or "guaranteed rankings." There are **no
  guaranteed outcomes**.
- **No fabricated proof.** No invented results, testimonials, logos, or case
  studies. We have no referenceable clients to name.
- **Budget Wheels is sandbox only** — referenced only as `budget_wheels_demo` /
  Tenant Zero for demos, never as a real client unless consent is confirmed.
- Listen first. Qualify before pitching.

## Pre-call checklist

- Dealership size and rough monthly lead volume.
- Current lead sources (web forms, marketplace, walk-in, phone).
- CRM in use and who owns it.
- Who owns marketing and who owns sales follow-up.
- Decision-maker and budget reality.

## Opening (neutral, non-promissory)

> "We run a controlled lead-to-appointment workflow for dealerships. The point
> isn't volume promises — it's that every step has a consent check, a human
> approval, and a record of what happened, so you can see exactly how each lead
> was handled. I'd like to understand how you handle leads today before saying
> whether it's a fit."

## Discovery questions

- How quickly does a new inbound lead get a first response today?
- Where do leads fall through — first response, follow-up, or booking?
- How do you track consent for calls/texts/emails to customers?
- What does your CRM capture, and what's manual?
- Who decides on a tool like this, and what's a realistic monthly budget?

## Positioning talk-track

- **Primary:** Demandara Sales Closer — lead intake → consent gate → human
  approval → appointment/CRM writeback → proof record → monthly proof report.
- **Differentiator (Cognitia):** the consent gate, human-approval gate, and the
  proof records. During the pilot this runs as a **pilot control model and a
  sandbox/demo proof workflow**, with a **monthly proof reporting deliverable
  subject to the signed pilot scope** — not a claim of finished production proof
  infrastructure.
- **Demo honesty:** the demo uses **sandbox appointment/CRM writeback**
  (`budget_wheels_demo` / Tenant Zero). **Live CRM/customer writes require
  written authorization and a scoped integration** — the sandbox demo is not a
  live deliverable.
- **Optional only:** Hermes Vision content/QC + privacy scan, if they want
  social/video content support. Supporting, not the core offer.

## Handling pricing

- Present three CAD tiers: Starter $500–$750, **Recommended $1,000–$1,500**,
  Managed Growth $1,500–$2,500.
- Lead with the **default first offer: CAD $1,000/mo for 90 days**.
- **Ads are out of scope.** If added later, **ad spend is paid directly by the
  dealer, never fronted by Cognitia.**
- Full detail: [`unit-economics-model.md`](./unit-economics-model.md) and
  [`client-zero-pilot-offer.md`](./client-zero-pilot-offer.md).

## Objection handling (honest)

- *"Can you guarantee leads/sales?"* — No. We don't promise outcomes. What we
  give you is a governed workflow and a monthly proof report of exactly what was
  done.
- *"Is this just AI spam?"* — No. Nothing goes out without a consent check and a
  human approval; every action is recorded.
- *"What about our customers' privacy/consent?"* — The consent gate runs before
  any outbound message, and live customer-data handling only happens with your
  written authorization. The optional content add-on also runs a privacy/PII
  scan.

## Qualification / next step

Good-fit signals: real inbound lead volume, a follow-up gap they feel, a
reachable decision-maker, and budget in range. **Next step is an agreed
follow-up — not a signed commitment in this call.**
