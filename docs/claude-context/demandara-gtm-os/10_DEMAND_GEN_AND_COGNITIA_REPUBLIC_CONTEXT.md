# 10 — Demand Gen and Cognitia Republic Context

Cognitia Republic is the content/demand/founder acquisition engine. It turns real build evidence into claim-safe education, proof-led content, and founder-led demand generation.

## Role in Demandara

Demand Gen should feed the GTM OS with:

- SEO/AEO/AIO page opportunities;
- proof-led content ideas;
- vertical explainer scripts;
- demo walkthroughs;
- before/after bottleneck narratives;
- claim-safe case-study templates.

## Content boundary

Use only evidence-backed claims. No fake revenue claims, fake customer claims, unsupported income language, or public statements about untested production behavior.

## Demandara demand engine modules

- keyword/opportunity registry;
- vertical pain library;
- proof-backed page generator;
- claim-safety checker;
- founder script generator;
- content-to-lead route tracker;
- monthly report summarizer.

## First vertical content theme

Used-car dealer wedge: missed leads, slow follow-up, trust gap around vehicle proof, manual BDC overload, and monthly proof report as owner-level accountability.
