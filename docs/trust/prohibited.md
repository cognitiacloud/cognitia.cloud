# What Remains Prohibited

> Hard rules for anyone running the Demandara/Cognitia demo or writing collateral
> from this pack. Scoped to the **current Hermes Vision checkout**. If a line
> below would be broken, **don't say it** — use the safe phrasing instead.

| # | Prohibited | Why | Say instead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Naming or implying any **customer** (names, logos, testimonials, case studies). | There are no customers in this checkout. | "First pilot is a template; no customers yet." |
| 2 | Quoting any **metric / number** (conversion, revenue, accuracy, uptime, growth). | No metrics exist or are measured here. | "We're not showing metrics; here is the control behavior live." |
| 3 | **Token / financial hype** (price, returns, APY, "to the moon", tokenomics, "guaranteed" gains). | Out of scope and misleading. | Describe the trust control, not financial upside. |
| 4 | "**SOC2 certified**" / "SOC2 compliant" / "audited". | Not certified, no audit, no report. | "Not certified. Early SOC2-readiness preparation for the Hermes Vision control surface only." |
| 5 | Claiming **C2PA / content provenance is shipping**. | Research direction only; not implemented. | "Provenance is a planned direction, not shipping." |
| 6 | Presenting **proof-governed GTM workflow/engine as implemented**. | Only Hermes Vision exists here; the workflow is planned/separate branch. | "This is the proof-governed GTM **motion / demo framing**; the engine is planned (separate branch)." |
| 7 | Implying a **live Client Zero** exists or is running. | It's a pilot template only. | "Client Zero is our pilot **checklist/template** for the first engagement." |
| 8 | Presenting **Budget Wheels** as live. | Sandbox construct. | "`budget_wheels_demo` runs in **Tenant Zero sandbox unless the founder confirms consent**." |
| 9 | Showing **real PII / secrets** in demo assets. | The skill is designed to *block* this; demoing with real data contradicts it. | Use the repo's **synthetic** `test_assets/` only. |
| 10 | Calling the Trust Center an **automated portal**. | It's a static draft. | "Trust Center **draft**." |
| 11 | Using the word "**guarantee**" for anything except the literal **no-post** behavior. | Avoid implied warranties. | "The skill never posts externally — that's enforced in code." |

## Enforcement

- Every external-facing claim must trace to the
  [claim-provenance table](./claim-provenance.md).
- Anything not marked **REAL** in [real-vs-mock.md](./real-vs-mock.md) must be
  spoken as **mock / sandbox / planned**.
- When unsure, omit the claim.
